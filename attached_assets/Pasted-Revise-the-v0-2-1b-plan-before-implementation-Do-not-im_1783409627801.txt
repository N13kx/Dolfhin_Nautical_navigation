==================================================

Do not automatically use only:

- chartedValueMetres

==================================================
4. CORRECT "RAW-PRESERVING" TERMINOLOGY
==================================================

Do not claim ogr2ogr GeoJSON is the raw S-57 representation.

Use this distinction:

immutable raw source truth:
- original .000 bytes
- verified SHA-256

decoded intermediate:
- GDAL/S57-decoded geometry and attributes
- reader version and options recorded

normalized output:
- Dolphin model

Rename the conceptual stage to:

"reader-preserving decoded intermediate"

or equivalent.

State explicitly that unknown GDAL-decoded attributes are preserved, but the intermediate is not claimed to preserve the original ISO/IEC 8211 record structure byte-for-byte.
